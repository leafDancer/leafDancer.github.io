01 / The research question

A benchmark built around the research workflow.

Economic research connects knowledge, literature, formal models, analysis, and writing. An agentic research system relies on its foundation model at every stage. Where can that model carry the work, and where does it need support?

AER-bench turns the intellectual core of this workflow into seven measurable tasks. Its tool-free baselines expose stage-specific strengths and weaknesses, providing a reference for building and evaluating research agents.

02 / Inside the benchmark

Seven stages. Different demands.

Select a task to explore what the model receives, what it produces, and how the answer is evaluated.

EK/189 evaluation items

Economic Knowledge

Does the model know the methods, findings, and institutional context that economic research depends on?

The task
Answer paper-specific questions in single-choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, and ordering formats.
Evaluation
Rule-based matching for factual answers, with normalized Kendall’s tau for ordering questions.
Evaluation protocol

Zero-shot, tool-free inference. EK and CI use rule-based scoring; the five generative tasks use dimension-level rubrics averaged across Claude 4.6 Sonnet and GPT-5.2. LR also includes citation recall.

03 / What we learned

Formal strength.
Fragile knowledge.

Models perform strongly on mathematical derivation, while literature review remains a bottleneck. Economic knowledge varies widely, identifying where research agents need retrieval and citation support.

56.0%best literature-review score
25.6–76.3%economic-knowledge range

Model families have distinct fingerprints.

GPT-5 variants lead mathematical modeling. Claude models show a stable advantage in concept identification. These profiles support choosing models for individual research stages.

Historical understanding is uneven.

Earlier economic texts show an aggregate gap of about 5 percentage points. After controlling for text length, the era effect is smaller and not statistically significant (+2.75 pp, p = 0.442).
